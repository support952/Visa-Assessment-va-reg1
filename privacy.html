This Privacy Policy explains how Immigration Advice Service ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you visit this website or submit a visa assessment. We take your privacy seriously and only collect what we need to evaluate your immigration options.

1. Who we are

Immigration Advice Service provides advisory visa assessments and immigration guidance to individuals, families, professionals, and investors interested in U.S. immigration pathways. For the purposes of data protection law (including the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation), we act as the data controller of the personal information you submit through this site.

You can reach our team at support@immigrationadviceservice.org for any privacy-related question.

2. Information we collect

We collect personal information directly from you when you complete the Visa Assessment form, contact us by email, or otherwise interact with this website. The categories of information we may collect include:

Identity & contact information

  • Full name
  • Email address and phone number
  • Country of residence and nationality
  • Date of birth or age range

Background information relevant to your visa assessment

  • Education and professional qualifications
  • Work experience and employment history
  • Business or investment background (where relevant)
  • Family status (spouse, dependents)
  • Immigration goals and timing preferences
  • Any prior visa applications, refusals, or immigration history you choose to share

Technical information

  • IP address, browser type, device type, operating system
  • Pages viewed, referring URL, time spent on the site
  • Cookie identifiers and similar technologies (see Cookies)
We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data (such as health, religion, or political views). Please do not submit such information through our forms or messages unless it is strictly relevant to your immigration question.

3. How we use your information

We process your personal information for the following purposes:

  • Visa Assessment. To review your profile and provide a personalized evaluation of suitable U.S. visa options.
  • Communication. To respond to inquiries, send your assessment results, schedule consultations, and follow up on your file.
  • Service delivery. If you proceed with immigration support, to manage your engagement, file preparation, and related tasks.
  • Site operation and security. To run, maintain, and secure the website, prevent abuse, and analyze traffic.
  • Legal compliance. To comply with applicable legal, regulatory, and reporting obligations.
  • Marketing (only with your consent). To send relevant updates or content where you have opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with similar laws, we rely on the following legal bases:

5. Sharing and third-party processors

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with trusted service providers acting on our instructions and bound by appropriate confidentiality and data-protection commitments. These currently include:

  • Typeform — to collect and store your Visa Assessment responses. See the Typeform Privacy Policy.
  • Hosting & infrastructure — to deliver and maintain this website on secure cloud infrastructure.
  • Email and communication tools — to send and receive correspondence related to your assessment.
  • Analytics — where used, to understand site usage in aggregate form.

We may also disclose information when required by law, court order, or regulatory authority, or where necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website uses a minimal set of cookies and similar technologies, including:

  • Essential cookies required for the site to function correctly.
  • Embedded Typeform components may set cookies in your browser when you open the assessment form.
  • Analytics cookies (where active) help us understand which pages are viewed most. These are loaded only with your consent where required.

You can control or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may impair certain features of the site.

7. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as needed to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by law:

  • Assessment leads — typically up to 24 months from your last interaction, unless you ask us to delete the data sooner.
  • Engaged clients — for the duration of the engagement plus the period required by applicable law and professional record-keeping standards.
  • Marketing data — until you withdraw consent.
  • Technical logs — for a short rolling period for security and diagnostics.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), access controls, and trusted processors. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security but commit to addressing incidents promptly.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we process your data.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, where processing relies on consent.
  • Complaint — lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@immigrationadviceservice.org. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law (generally within one month).

California residents have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of any "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

10. International data transfers

We and our processors may store and process information in countries outside your country of residence, including the United States and the European Union. Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA or the UK, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms to ensure adequate protection.

11. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or for other operational reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised. Material changes will be highlighted clearly.

13. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your personal information, contact us at:
