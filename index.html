What Is a Visa Assessment?

Choosing the wrong visa path can waste months of time, thousands of dollars, and lead to unnecessary refusals.

A Visa Assessment helps you understand exactly which U.S. immigration options fit your background, qualifications, experience, and goals — before you start the application process.

We carefully review your profile and provide a personalized immigration evaluation so you can move forward with confidence and clarity, focusing only on the pathway that is most realistic and effective for you.

What happens next: Once you have completed the form, one of our immigration agents will contact you from a U.S. (+1) or local number, depending on your region.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the Visa Assessment?
We review your qualifications, work experience, financial background, immigration goals, and other important factors to identify the U.S. visa options that best fit your profile.
Will I find out exactly which visa I qualify for?
We provide a professional evaluation of the visa pathways that appear most suitable and realistic for your situation.
Is the assessment legally binding?
No. The assessment is an advisory evaluation designed to help you understand your immigration options before starting the formal process.
Can you help after the assessment?
Yes. If you decide to move forward, we can assist with the visa application and the full immigration process.
How long does the assessment take?
Timeframes vary depending on the complexity of your profile, but most assessments are completed quickly once we receive all required information.
What if I don't currently qualify for a visa?
If your current profile isn't strong enough, we may still identify future strategies or steps that could improve your eligibility later.
Is the assessment only for work visas?
No. We assess many different immigration pathways — including business, investor, employment, family, and long-term residency options.
Can families apply together?
Yes. We evaluate family-based relocation strategies and explain which options may include spouses and children.
Why shouldn't I just research online myself?
Online information is often confusing, outdated, or too general. A professional assessment gives you personalized guidance based on your exact situation.
What happens after I submit my request?
Our team reviews your information, evaluates your eligibility, and provides guidance on the most suitable immigration pathways available to you.
