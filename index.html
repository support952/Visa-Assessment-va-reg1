FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers to the questions every U.S. visa applicant asks first.

What happens after I complete the form?

Once you have completed the form, one of our immigration agents will contact you from a U.S. (+1) or local number, depending on your region. We typically reach out within one business day to schedule your assessment.

How long does the visa assessment take?

Most assessments are completed within a few business days of receiving your full profile. Complex investor or corporate cases may take longer, and we will tell you upfront if that's the case.

Is the initial consultation really free?

Yes. Your initial eligibility assessment is provided at no cost and with no obligation. Any optional case-management or filing services are quoted clearly in advance — never charged without your written approval.

Will you tell me if I don't qualify for any U.S. visa?

Always. Honesty is the foundation of our practice. If no current visa fits your profile, we tell you directly and outline what would need to change for you to qualify in the future.

How do you protect my personal information?

All communications are encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard protocols. Our data-handling practices are GDPR and CCPA compliant. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Can my spouse and children be included in my application?

Most U.S. visa categories allow derivative status for a spouse and unmarried children under 21. We assess your full family unit as part of every consultation.
